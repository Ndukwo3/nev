About me

I’m Ndukwo Emmanuel Victor, a multidisciplinary designer and creative technologist focused on turning ideas and problems into meaningful digital experiences and products. My work sits at the intersection of UI/UX design, product design, visual design, technology, and AI.

I started my journey in graphic design and have grown into exploring how design and technology can work together to create useful, intuitive, and engaging products. From designing interfaces and visual identities to building software, experimenting with AI, and developing digital products, I enjoy taking ideas from concept to reality.

I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and exploring new ways people can interact with technology. My goal is to create work that is not only visually compelling but also purposeful, functional, and capable of solving real-world problems.

Toolbox: Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, CorelDRAW, HTML/CSS/JS, Next.js, TypeScript, Python, C#, .NET, Flutter, AI tools, and other emerging technologies.
